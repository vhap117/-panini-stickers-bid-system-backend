# The system stops at "sold" — no payment or delivery handling

When a seller accepts a Bid, the Listing becomes `sold`, other Bids are rejected, and bidders are notified. The system's responsibility ends there. Payment, shipping, and the physical exchange of stickers are handled outside the app — cash on meetup, an in-person swap, or whatever the seller and winning bidder arrange in the Chat Room. We chose this over building payment/escrow/shipping because a large portion of Panini trading is in-person swaps with no money involved, and adding payment processing would multiply scope and risk (payment provider integration, escrow logic, dispute resolution, refunds) for a use case that's often a handshake. The backend's value is finding the match (feed, geo-ranking) and facilitating negotiation (bids, chat), not being a payment processor. The trade-off is no platform revenue from transaction fees and no protection against a user flaking on a meetup — acceptable for v1 of a social app where trust is social, not enforced by escrow. Payment can be added later for long-distance monetary sales if the product demands it.
